03 · Featured Research

Final Year Project — LLM factuality evaluation

★ 1st Runner-Up — Best Project (Data Science)

Comparative evaluation of factual reliability in large language models under paraphrased prompts

Designed a multi-metric benchmark comparing ChatGPT-4o mini, DeepSeek-V3.2, and Gemini 3 Flash across 817 TruthfulQA questions in 38 categories using original and paraphrased prompts. The evaluation assessed factual accuracy, hallucination, consistency, and paraphrase sensitivity.

817TruthfulQA questions
38question categories
3LLMs evaluated
4reliability metrics
2×original + paraphrased prompts

Evaluation framework

Factual accuracyWhether answers matched the benchmark's correct reference set.
Hallucination rateHow often confident answers were false or unsupported.
ConsistencyWhether answers remained stable after paraphrasing.
Paraphrase sensitivityHow much model accuracy changed when wording changed.

Results

ModelAccuracyHallucinationLabel consistencyParaphrase sensitivityOverall
ChatGPT-4o mini81.70%9.85%90.33%0.61 pp#1 · 62.65
Gemini 3 Flash82.86%7.53%87.27%1.22 pp#2 · 60.93
DeepSeek-V3.278.52%8.69%78.46%7.71 pp#3 · 56.59
Automated response evaluation using RapidFuzz lexical matching, SentenceTransformers semantic similarity, and rule-based classification to support consistent benchmarking across models and prompt variants.
Key finding: Gemini 3 Flash achieved the highest factual accuracy and lowest hallucination rate, while ChatGPT-4o mini ranked best overall because it was the most consistent and least sensitive to paraphrasing. The study shows that reliable AI evaluation should consider robustness and consistency alongside accuracy.
